<h3>Advanced Beam Analyser</h3>
<br>
Choose your support type.
<br>
Then enter your length of beam in m.
<br>
Then define the load and point from which load is defined.
<br>
Press the button add load to add the load in the sequence.
<br>
Then click on Generate Diagram to see the SFD, BMD and the deflection curve.
<br>
Below you can see the max/min values of shear force, and bending moment.
<br>
also you can save the image in your galary by downloading it in PNG or CSV format to show it in your project file.
